# Some questions need another description.

A model can answer every question its language permits and still miss the relation the problem needs. Causality asks how descriptions can change without losing account of what they preserve, forget and make possible.

![A dark field of teal points with faint currents curving through it.](https://causality.in/assets/renditions/relation-field/scene-0.webp)

<a id="summary"></a>

<a id="when-the-frame-becomes-too-small"></a>

## When the frame becomes too small.

More accurate estimates cannot supply a distinction the representation cannot express. MONAD seeks a calculus for changing that representation, including the questions and transformations it admits.

![A dense field of blue-white points threaded with fine curving lines.](https://causality.in/assets/renditions/relation-field/scene-1.webp)

<a id="distinction"></a>

<a id="experience-should-change-what-comes-next"></a>

## Experience should change what comes next.

A useful memory is more than a growing record. Reckon studies which experience changes later outcomes, what deserves to survive a finite budget and how to examine the difference.

![Teal points scattered around a darker, emptier centre.](https://causality.in/assets/renditions/relation-field/scene-2.webp)

<a id="relation"></a>

<a id="intelligence-between-the-parts"></a>

## Intelligence between the parts.

MUON investigates the adaptive local unit. HENA asks how many such units can learn to communicate, remember and specialise, making their organisation part of the computation.

![Golden points gathered into one dense, ring-like cloud.](https://causality.in/assets/renditions/relation-field/scene-3.webp)

<a id="compatibility"></a>

<a id="an-organisation-that-can-learn"></a>

## An organisation that can learn.

[Pale](https://rawthoughts.in/pale/) makes responsibilities, capabilities and coordination explicit, and HENA explores whether more of that organisation could arise from intent through local interactions, a direction [the About page](https://causality.in/about/) sets out.

![Sparse rose points carried along long, sweeping currents.](https://causality.in/assets/renditions/relation-field/scene-4.webp)

## Another substrate. The same ambition.

Q-MUON and Q-HENA take the local-unit and collective questions into quantum computation. Classical and quantum paths develop alongside each other, exploring what each can contribute.

![Cyan points with brighter currents winding through the dark.](https://causality.in/assets/renditions/relation-field/scene-5.webp)
