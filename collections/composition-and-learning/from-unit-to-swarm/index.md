Title: From unit to swarm

[Read HTML](https://causality.in/collections/composition-and-learning/from-unit-to-swarm/)

Author: Harsh Joshi

How much of a collective answer belongs to its parts, and how much to their arrangement? We need a usable component boundary before that question can be tested. Even then, individually adequate units can lose a joint fact or compete for the same capacity.

The progression therefore builds from substitution to composition before asking what learned organisation contributes. Otherwise a swarm's apparent gain could come from a stronger model hidden in one slot, or its failure from a broken component rather than the routing being studied. Keeping those alternatives visible gives the organisational experiment a particular problem to solve.
