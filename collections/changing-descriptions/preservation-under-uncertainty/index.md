Title: Preservation under uncertainty

[Read HTML](https://causality.in/collections/changing-descriptions/preservation-under-uncertainty/)

An observation can leave uncertainty and still support a useful decision. The promise then depends on the error we permit, the source of another observation and the transformations the evidence undergoes. Exact recovery, a bounded decision risk and closeness to a previous description are different successes.

The distinction matters over a sequence of changes. Repeating a measurement can supply new evidence; processing one report cannot do the same job. A simulator can preserve a decision well enough without reproducing the original experiment. A locally acceptable replacement can also carry a chain beyond its first tolerance. These are related questions about what a description preserves, but answering one does not settle the others.
